ABOUT GREAT GENERATION.

Building discipline, developing character and encouraging leadership for a greater generation.

Our identity

More than a camp. A foundation for growth.

Great Generation Disciplinary Camp is based in Witbank, Emalahleni, Mpumalanga, South Africa.

Our camp focuses on discipline, leadership and physical training for participants aged 13 to 35.

We aim to create an environment where participants can develop personal responsibility, confidence, respect and a stronger commitment to their goals.

Our philosophy is built around one message: Discipline Today • Leaders Tomorrow.
