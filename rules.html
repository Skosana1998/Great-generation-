RULES & REGULATIONS

Discipline begins with responsibility. Every participant is expected to respect the camp, fellow participants, instructors and the values that Great Generation stands for.

IMPORTANT NOTICE

These rules are established to maintain a safe, respectful, structured and supportive training environment. All participants must understand and follow the camp regulations throughout their programme.

01. Respect & Behaviour

  • Respect all instructors, management and fellow participants.
  • No bullying, intimidation, harassment or discrimination.
  • Use respectful language at all times.
  • Follow reasonable and lawful instructions from camp staff.
  • Respect personal boundaries and individual differences.

02. Attendance & Punctuality

  • Participants must attend scheduled activities.
  • Arrive on time for training sessions and meetings.
  • No leaving activities without permission.
  • Report illness, injury or emergencies immediately.
  • Absences must be explained to camp management.

03. Accommodation & Cleanliness

  • Keep sleeping areas and personal spaces clean.
  • Respect camp property and accommodation facilities.
  • Maintain personal hygiene.
  • Respect other participants' belongings and privacy.
  • Follow designated sleeping and resting schedules.

04. Prohibited Substances

  • No illegal drugs or controlled substances.
  • No alcohol during camp activities.
  • No smoking or vaping in prohibited areas.
  • No possession or distribution of prohibited substances.
  • Any substance testing must follow applicable law and consent requirements.

05. Safety & Physical Training

  • Follow all safety instructions during activities.
  • Do not participate in dangerous or unauthorised activities.
  • Inform staff about injuries or medical concerns.
  • No physical punishment, assault or forced exercise.
  • Participants must be allowed appropriate rest, hydration and medical assistance.

06. Personal Belongings & Property

  • Take responsibility for personal belongings.
  • No stealing or damaging property.
  • Do not enter restricted areas without permission.
  • Report lost or damaged property to management.
  • Respect all equipment and facilities.

07. Mobile Phones & Communication

  • Follow the camp's agreed mobile phone arrangements.
  • Use devices responsibly.
  • No recording or sharing another participant's images without permission.
  • Emergency communication with guardians must remain available.
  • Respect privacy and confidentiality.

08. Conduct & Disciplinary Procedures

  • Minor rule violations may result in counselling or a verbal warning.
  • Repeated violations may result in a formal warning.
  • Serious misconduct will be investigated fairly.
  • Participants must have an opportunity to explain their actions.
  • Parents or guardians must be involved where appropriate, especially for minors.
  • Serious safety concerns may require immediate protective action.

09. Parent & Guardian Responsibilities

  • Provide accurate participant information.
  • Complete required registration and consent documentation.
  • Disclose relevant medical and emergency information.
  • Provide accurate emergency contact details.
  • Remain available for communication concerning minors.

IMPORTANT: ZERO TOLERANCE FOR ABUSE

Great Generation Disciplinary Camp does not permit physical abuse, sexual abuse, humiliation, degrading treatment, threats or exploitation. Participant safety, dignity and wellbeing must always come first.

PARTICIPANT ACKNOWLEDGEMENT

By registering for a programme, participants and, where applicable, their parents or legal guardians acknowledge that they have been provided with the camp rules and understand the importance of following them.

Registration does not waive any legal rights or remove the camp's duty to provide a safe environment.

DISCIPLINE. RESPECT. RESPONSIBILITY.

Become part of a generation built on strong values.
