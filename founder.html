Our Founder

MJ Zwane

CEO & Founder

MJ Zwane is the CEO and Founder of Great Generation Disciplinary Camp, an initiative built around the importance of discipline, leadership and personal development.

Great Generation Disciplinary Camp aims to create an environment where young people and adults can develop discipline, strengthen their character and build a positive approach to life.

Through structured physical training, leadership development and discipline-focused activities, the camp seeks to encourage participants to recognise their potential and take responsibility for their future.

The vision is simple: help build a generation that understands the value of commitment, respect, responsibility and leadership.

“Discipline Today • Leaders Tomorrow.”
